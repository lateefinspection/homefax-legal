HomeFax Privacy Policy

HomeFax respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

SMS Communications

If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in to receive text messages from HomeFax, we may send recurring SMS messages related to your account, inspection review, home-care tasks, store reminders, monitoring alerts, and service-related updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP, and you may reply HELP for help. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile opt-in information and phone numbers collected for SMS messaging will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Questions about HomeFax SMS notifications can be sent to notifications@homefaxai.cloud.
